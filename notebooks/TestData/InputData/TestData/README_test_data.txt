This folder contains pseudo-OCV curves in both charge and discharge direction
for a Molicel P45B cell cycled between 0% and 100% SOC for 800 equivalent full
cycles.

The cell is one of the four cells used in the DMA framework publication:

M. Rehm et al., "How to determine the degradation modes of lithium-ion
batteries with silicon-graphite blend electrodes," Journal of Power Sources,
vol. 670, 239418, 2026.
DOI: 10.1016/j.jpowsour.2026.239418
